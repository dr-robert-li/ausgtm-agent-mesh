|----------|-------------|
| make use-* swap → active config | `make use-*` overwrites config/model_gateway.config.yaml (the active profile build_router reads by default). A wrong restore source would leave a stale/cloud config active. |

Mitigation here is that `use-vllm`/`use-ollama` swap in ONLY the egress-free Plan-01 profiles (whose loopback-only, cloud-ref-free content is grep-asserted in Plan 01), `use-cloud` restores from the pristine cloud anchor via `cp` (not `git checkout`, Pitfall 5), and `run-vllm`/`run-ollama` are best-effort operator-only targets explicitly kept out of `make test`/CI (D-07) — asserted by the Task-1 acceptance check that the `test:` recipe is unchanged. The LOCAL-04 test (Task 2) further asserts every swapped-in profile's api_base is loopback and free of cloud markers, constructing build_router with no network. |
